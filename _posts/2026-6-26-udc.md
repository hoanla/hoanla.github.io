Just wrapped up two inspiring weeks at the [Social Computing and Computational Social Science (SICSS-UDC) Summer Institute](https://sites.google.com/view/sicss-udc2026/home?authuser=0), hosted by the University of the District of Columbia.

I joined the program hoping to learn new computational methods, but I left with much more than that. The sessions challenged me to think more deeply about research design, ethics, and the value of interdisciplinary collaboration.

A heartfelt thank you to the UDC team - Tayler, Andrea Adams, Cotina, Angelyn, Hanna, Mariama, and Aidan — for creating such a welcoming and engaging learning environment. Your hard work and support made this an incredible experience.

I'm also grateful to all the speakers and fellow participants. I learned so much from your presentations, questions, and conversations, and I look forward to staying connected!
