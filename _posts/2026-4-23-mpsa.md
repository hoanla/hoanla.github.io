Another great trip to Chicago for the 2026 Midwest Political Science Association (MPSA) Annual Conference! This year was especially busy and rewarding—I presented two of my research papers and also served as a discussant for a panel. It was a great opportunity to share my work, receive helpful feedback, learn from other scholars, and contribute to conversations about new research.

I am very grateful to the Center for Public Service at Texas Tech University for sponsoring my travel and making my participation in MPSA 2026 possible.
