Another great trip to Chicago for the 2026 Midwest Political Science Association (MPSA) Annual Conference! This year was especially busy and rewarding. I presented two of my research papers and also served as a discussant for a panel. It was a great opportunity to share my work, receive and give helpful feedback, learn from other scholars, and contribute to conversations about new research.

Of course, conferences are also about the people. It was so nice to catch up with old friends, meet some new ones, and enjoy some really good food together. And I have to say, Chicago has some of the best Asian food!

I am very grateful to the Center for Public Service at Texas Tech University for sponsoring my travel and making my participation in MPSA 2026 possible.
