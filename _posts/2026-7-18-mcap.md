Another summer, another campus to visit and learn! I was so grateful for the opportunity to spend a week at Purdue University geeking out over longitudinal data analysis at [the MCAP Longitudinal Data Analysis Summer Institute](https://www.purdue.edu/research/mcap/summer-institute/).

It was definitely an intense week of learning, but also such a fun one. I got to learn alongside an amazing group of people from different disciplines, share lots of ideas, laugh a lot, and somehow make some really great friends along the way. That might be one of my favorite parts of these summer programs---you come for the methods and leave with new people in your life too!

A big thank you to the MCAP team for organizing such a fantastic program and making the whole week both productive and genuinely enjoyable. I learned a lot, met some wonderful people, and had a great time at Purdue. Another summer week very well spent!
